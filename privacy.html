Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

DeToro Systems ("we," "us," or "our") is a sole proprietorship owned by Jacob DeToro, located at 8280 W Highlander Dr, Mequon, WI 53097. We set up missed-call text-back and lead follow-up systems for local remodelers and home service contractors. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and services and how we use it.

Information we collect

This website collects personal information in two places: the chat widget (name, mobile phone number and message) and the contact form (name, company, email address, phone number and message). We collect only what you choose to enter. We also receive any information you include if you email, call or text us directly.

When we provide text-back services to a business client, we handle the phone numbers and messages of people who call that business. We use that information only to deliver the service the client hired us for.

How we use information

  • To reply to your question or request by text, phone or email.
  • To set up and run the text-back services our clients hire us for.
  • To communicate with our clients about their service.

Text messaging and mobile information

If you give us your mobile number through the chat widget, or text or call our business number, you agree to receive text messages from DeToro Systems about your inquiry. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share information only as needed to provide our services (for example, with the telecommunications and messaging providers that deliver text messages), to comply with the law, or to protect our rights.

Data retention

We keep information only as long as we need it to provide our services and meet legal obligations. After that, we delete or de-identify it.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any text message to opt out, or HELP for help. You can also contact us to ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information.

Children's privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post changes on this page and update the date above.
