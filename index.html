One short call, then I handle it

You keep your number and your phone. Setup takes about 10 minutes of your time.

Works with the phone you already have

Cell phone Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile and most others
We turn on forward-when-unanswered with a short code from your phone. It takes about 2 minutes, and one code turns it off.
Office landline
Most landline companies offer forward-on-no-answer. We call your provider together to switch it on. Some charge a small monthly fee.
Business phone app RingCentral, Grasshopper, Google Voice and similar
We set your text-back line as the destination for unanswered calls in your app's settings.

Callers can still leave a voicemail, and it shows up in your app. Not sure what you have? We figure it out on the setup call.

Questions owners ask

How long until it's live?

Usually about 2 weeks. The setup call takes 10 minutes. Then the phone carriers have to approve your business for texting, which typically takes 1 to 3 weeks. Your 30 free days don't start until your texts are live.

How fast does the text go out?

Within a minute of the call going unanswered, once your line is live.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. Calls you don't answer forward to a local text-enabled line, and the text goes out under your business name. Your number stays on your truck, your website and your Google listing.

Can I change what the text says?

Yes. You approve the wording before it goes live, and you can change it any time by texting me.

What if I'm already talking to that customer?

They get one short, friendly text, the same as any missed caller. If they don't need it, they ignore it. Anyone can reply STOP to stop getting texts.
